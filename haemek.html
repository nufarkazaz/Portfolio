HaEmek Student Housing

First Year Academic Project

A residential housing complex designed for international exchange students

Designed as an homage to kibbutz life, this complex offers visiting exchange students an authentic Israeli experience—combining rural heritage with modern additions, set along a village-style pathway system inspired by Emek Hefer's settlements.

  • Location: Ruppin Academic College, Israel
  • Role: Architectural Design & Interior Planning
