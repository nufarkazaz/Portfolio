Shitah Kindergarten Complex

Second Year Academic Project

An educational complex combining kindergarten classrooms, activity hubs, and therapeutic spaces for learning, speech, and developmental support.

Inspired by a children’s treehouse, the project steps down the hillside to bridge three elevation levels while drawing inspiration from the natural landscape of Ramat HaNadiv.

  • Location: Zichron Ya'acov, Israel
  • Role: Architectural Design & Interior Planning
