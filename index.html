Shitah Kindergarten Complex

Sensory-Driven Educational Environment & Biophilic Design
Located in Zichron Ya'akov near Ramat Hanadiv, this three-classroom complex incorporates biophilic architecture inspired by the surrounding landscape. Designed around an existing native olive tree to create a "treehouse" experience, the stepped structure maximizes natural views and incorporates a dedicated sensory garden engaging smell, taste, and texture.

"HaEmek" Student Housing

Rural-Inspired Student Residence
Designed to accommodate international exchange students at Ruppin College, "HaEmek" is meant to create an authentic Israeli rural experience. The project integrates local agricultural heritage with contemporary design, blending traditional village architecture with modern elements to connect the past with the present.
